## What happened

In "Sestava brány" (P282) I made the gate frame I60 rigid onto the hinge leaf inside a placed sub-assembly: snap_faces {type: rigid, move: {part: I60}, onto: {part: I37, face: "I14/F1:f5"}} (I37 = Sestava pant L, its inner I14 leaf turns on the hinge pin, motion "Otáčení pantu"). drive_mechanism of the hinge 180° -> 170°: the leaf turned, but I60 was only shifted by ~6 mm (position 64.6,-292.5 -> 67.6,-287.2), its orientation and bbox size unchanged (40 x 340), so the gate does not swing with the leaf.

## What was expected

A rigid joint onto a sub-assembly's inner part keeps the full relative transform: the frame turns with the leaf about the hinge pin (it should move ~55 mm and rotate 10°).

## Steps

P276 / P277: axis joint leaf on pin + rigid screws; P282: remove_joint J1, place_part I37 fixed, snap_faces rigid I60 onto I37 face I14/F1:f5, drive_mechanism M2 to 170, get_assembly / top view screenshot.

## Workaround

Trying face joints (touch / axis) instead of rigid across the levels.
